How to sign a PDF with a digital certificate on your phone

A digital signature made with a certificate proves who signed a document and that it has not been changed since. With PDF Stroke you can sign from your phone or your browser, using a certificate from a certification authority (for example Spain’s FNMT or your national ID certificate).

What you need

  • Your digital certificate as a .p12 or .pfx file (with its private key) and its password.
  • If your certificate (for example, one from Spain’s FNMT) is installed in your computer’s browser, you can export it as a .p12/.pfx file “with the private key” from the browser’s certificate settings, and then copy it to your phone.

Steps to sign

  1. Open PDF Stroke and tap Digital signature on the home screen. Choose the PDF.
  2. Tap Choose certificate… and select your .p12 or .pfx file.
  3. Type the password in Certificate password and, if you like, a reason (for example, “Approved”).
  4. Leave Add a visible stamp on the last page checked if you want “Digitally signed by…” and the date to appear on that page.
  5. Tap Sign and save. A copy of the PDF with “(signed)” in its name is saved.
Privacy: your certificate and password are only used on your device to sign. They are never sent anywhere or stored.

Is the signature valid?

Yes: it is a standard PDF signature (PKCS#7 with SHA-256). You can verify it in Adobe Acrobat Reader or with your country’s official validation service. If you change anything in the PDF after signing it, the signature is no longer valid, which is exactly what should happen.

If you only need a handwritten signature, use the “Signature” tool and draw it with your finger.
