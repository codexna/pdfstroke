How to read a PDF aloud and use reading mode (free)

Listen to a document while you do something else, go over your notes or give your eyes a rest: PDF Stroke reads PDFs aloud and has a distraction-free, full-screen reading mode.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Read aloud

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Stroke.
  2. Open the ⇄ menu and tap Read aloud.
  3. The app reads sentence by sentence and highlights what it is reading. You can pause, skip forward or go back.
  4. You can change the voice and the speed (from 0.75× to 2×).
On Android, the app uses the system voices. If you install higher-quality voices in Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech output, the reading will sound more natural. On a computer, it uses the browser's voices.

Reading mode

  1. In the ⇄ menu, tap Reading mode.
  2. The document switches to full screen, with no bars or buttons.
  3. Choose a normal, sepia or night background for reading in low light.

The app remembers the page you were on, so when you open the document again you pick up right where you left off.

Does it work with scans?

If the PDF is a photo or a scan, you first need to recognize the text with Edit text (OCR). After that, it can be read aloud.
