How to add a watermark and page numbers to a PDF (free)

A watermark makes it clear that a document is a copy or a draft, and helps stop it from being reused for something else. Numbering the pages makes long documents easier to read and print. PDF Stroke does both.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Add a watermark

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Stroke.
  2. In the ⇄ menu, tap Watermark.
  3. Type the text, for example COPY, DRAFT or For rental application only.
  4. Choose the color, the opacity and whether it runs diagonally. It is applied to every page.
  5. Tap Save.
Tip for your ID card: when you send a copy of your ID card, add a watermark with the reason and the date. If anyone tried to use it for something else, it would be clear that it isn't valid.

Number the pages

  1. In the ⇄ menu, tap Number pages.
  2. Choose the position and the format: “1”, “- 1 -” or “Page 1 of 10”.
  3. Tap Save.
