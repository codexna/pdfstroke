How to reduce PDF file size (compress a PDF for free)

Is your PDF too big to send? It’s almost always because of the photos or scans inside it. PDF Stroke shrinks them without touching the text, right on your own device.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Steps to compress a PDF

  1. Open PDF Stroke and tap Compress PDF. Choose the document.
  2. Choose how much to compress:
    • Low: almost no quality loss.
    • Medium: the recommended level for email and messaging apps.
    • Maximum: the smallest file (photos look a little worse).
  3. Tap Compress. The app shows you the file size before and after, and saves a “(compressed)” copy.
A PDF with several photos usually ends up 50% to 80% smaller. If the PDF only contains text, it is already small and can hardly be made any smaller.

More tools
