How to scan documents with your phone and save them as a PDF

You don’t need a scanner to send a signed document, an invoice or your ID card. With your phone’s camera and PDF Stroke, you get a clean, straight PDF in just a few seconds.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Steps to scan a document

  1. Open PDF Stroke on your Android phone and tap Scan document.
  2. Point the camera at the page. The app detects the edges and takes the photo; you can also take it yourself.
  3. Adjust the crop if needed, and add more pages if the document has several.
  4. When you’re done, a PDF is created with all the pages, straightened and cropped.
  5. Tap Save or share it directly.
The camera scanner is part of the Android app. In your computer’s browser, you can use Image to PDF with photos you already have.

Tips for a good scan

  • Place the page on a dark, smooth surface: this makes the edges easier to detect.
  • Find good lighting and avoid casting your own shadow on the paper.
  • Hold your phone parallel to the page, not tilted.

Edit the scanned text

A scan is an image, but with Edit text the app recognizes the letters (OCR), so you can correct or copy the text just like in any other PDF. Afterwards you can sign it, protect it with a password or compress it before sending it.
