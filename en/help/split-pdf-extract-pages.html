How to split a PDF or extract specific pages (free)

Sometimes you only need to send two pages of a long document, or split payslips that arrive all together into separate files. PDF Stroke lets you extract pages or split a PDF in seconds.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Extract specific pages

  1. Open PDF Stroke, tap Extract pages and choose the PDF. The Extract or split pages window opens.
  2. Enter the pages you want, for example 1-3, 7, 10.
  3. Tap OK. A new PDF is saved with only those pages; the original stays unchanged.

Split a PDF into separate files

  1. In the same tool, choose Split: each page into a separate PDF (in a .zip).
  2. Tap OK. You get a .zip file containing one PDF per page, numbered in order.
Tip: if what you actually want is to remove pages, open the PDF, go to Organize pages, delete the ones you don’t need and save.

When it comes in handy

  • Sending only the signed page of a contract.
  • Separating invoices, payslips or receipts that come in a single PDF.
  • Making a document smaller by removing blank or unnecessary pages.

Frequently asked questions

Does the text stay selectable? Yes. The pages are copied without being turned into images, so the text, links and quality are preserved.
