How to convert a PDF to JPG or PNG (and photos to PDF)

Sometimes it’s handier to have a PDF page as an image: to post it on social media, drop it into a presentation or send it as a photo on WhatsApp. PDF Stroke turns PDF pages into JPG or PNG images, and it also works the other way round.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

From PDF to image

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Stroke.
  2. Open the ⇄ menu and choose PDF to image in JPG or PNG format.
  3. Each page is saved as a separate image, at a resolution good enough for viewing or printing.
JPG or PNG? JPG files are smaller and work well for photos and scanned documents. PNG keeps text and lines sharper and supports transparent backgrounds, but the files are larger.

From photos to PDF

  1. Tap Image to PDF on the home screen.
  2. Choose one or more photos. Each one becomes a page.
  3. If needed, reorder them in Organize pages, then tap Save.

If your photos are of paper documents, you’re better off using Scan document: it crops the edges and straightens the page so it looks properly scanned.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert just one page? Yes: first extract that page with Extract or split pages, then convert it.
