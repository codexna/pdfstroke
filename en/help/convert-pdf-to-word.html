How to convert a PDF to Word for free (and Word to PDF)

PDF Stroke converts a PDF into a Word document (.docx) that you can open and edit in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice, and it also works the other way around: from Word to PDF. Everything happens on your device.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

PDF to Word

  1. Open PDF Stroke and tap PDF to Word on the home screen.
  2. Choose the PDF. The app converts it into a .docx file with the text, its formatting and the images.
  3. The file is saved to Documents › PDF Stroke (on a computer, it is downloaded) and you can open it in Word.
If the PDF is already open, you can also convert it from the ⇄ menu › PDF to Word, including any changes you have made.

Word to PDF

  1. Tap Word to PDF and choose the .docx file.
  2. Check the result (you can edit it like any other PDF) and tap Save.

Images too

With Image to PDF you can combine several photos into one PDF, and with PDF to image you can save each page as a JPG or PNG. For paper documents, use Scan document: the camera detects the edges, straightens the page and turns it into a PDF.
