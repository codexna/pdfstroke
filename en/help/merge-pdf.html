How to merge multiple PDFs into one (free, without uploading)

Many forms and applications ask for “a single PDF”: both sides of your ID card, several invoices, or a contract with its appendices. With PDF Stroke you merge them into one file on your own device, without uploading them to any website.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Steps to merge PDFs

  1. Open PDF Stroke and tap Merge PDF on the home screen.
  2. Choose all the files you want to combine. You can mix PDFs, photos (JPG or PNG) and Word documents.
  3. A single document opens with all the pages, in the order you chose the files.
  4. To change the order, go to Organize pages and drag each thumbnail into place. There you can also rotate, duplicate or delete pages.
  5. Tap Save. The merged PDF is saved to Documents › PDF Stroke (on a computer, it is downloaded instead).
Already have a PDF open? Go to the ⇄ menu › Merge with other PDFs, images or Word to add more files to the end of the document you are editing.

Tips

  • If the final PDF is too large to send by email, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.
  • For your ID card or other paper documents, use Scan document first: the camera crops and straightens each page.
  • Password-protected PDFs can be merged as long as you know the password: remove it first with Remove password.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the number of files? There is no maximum; the only limit is your phone’s memory. With ordinary documents you can merge dozens of files without any problem.

Do I lose any quality? No. The pages of each PDF are copied exactly as they are, with their original text and images.
