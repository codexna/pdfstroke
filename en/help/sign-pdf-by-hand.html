How to sign a PDF on your phone with your finger (free)

To sign a school permission slip, a quote or a rental agreement, you don't need to print it, sign it and scan it. With PDF Stroke, you draw your signature with your finger and place it on the PDF.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Steps to sign

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Stroke.
  2. In the toolbar, tap Signature.
  3. Draw your signature with your finger (or with the mouse on a computer). You can choose the color and the thickness.
  4. Tap the spot on the page where the signature should go. Drag it to move it and use the corners to resize it.
  5. Tap Save.
Your signatures are saved on your device, so next time you just have to pick one.

Fill in the rest of the document

  • With Add text you can type your name, your ID card number or the date in the blanks.
  • With Edit text you can correct the text that was already in the PDF.
  • With Image you can add a scanned company stamp.

Handwritten or digital signature?

A drawn signature is fine for most everyday paperwork. For government procedures or documents that require you to identify yourself securely, use a digital signature with a certificate.
