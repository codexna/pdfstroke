How to edit the text of a PDF on your phone (free)

With PDF Stroke you can change the text that is already in a PDF (rather than pasting a patch over it) from your phone or from your computer’s browser, for free and without uploading the document to any server.

Get it free on Google Play Use it in your browser

Steps to edit the text

  1. Open PDF Stroke and tap Open PDF. Choose the document.
  2. In the bottom toolbar, tap Edit text. The lines you can edit are marked with a box.
  3. Tap the line you want to change and type. You can delete words, add new ones or rewrite the whole line.
  4. At the top you can change the font, size and color or make it bold or italic, even for just part of the text.
  5. To move the line, drag it by its handle. To delete it, tap the red trash can button.
  6. Tap Save. The PDF is saved to Documents › PDF Stroke (on a computer, it is downloaded).
Is it a scan or a photo? If the page is an image, tap it with “Edit text” and the app will recognize the text (OCR), so you can edit it in the same way. It works offline.

Other things you can do

  • Add new text anywhere with “Add text”.
  • Cover information (for example, an ID card number) with a rectangle in the background color: the text underneath is removed from the file, not just hidden.
  • Move, replace or delete images and logos in the PDF with “Select”.
  • Add signatures, drawings and notes, highlight text and organize the pages.
