PDF Stroke privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Your documents never leave your device. PDF Stroke has no user accounts and no analytics of its own.

Documents

  • The documents you open are processed only on your device, also when you convert them (PDF to Word or image, Word or images to PDF), merge them or recognize the text of a photo or scan. They are not sent to any server.
  • The PDFs you save are written to your device (Documents › PDF Stroke folder). They are only sent to another app if you choose to do so with the Share button.
  • The signatures you draw are remembered only on your device so you can reuse them. You can delete them from the app itself or by clearing the app's data.
  • For the digital signature with a certificate, the certificate file (.p12 / .pfx) and its password are used only on your device to sign the document: they are not sent to any server and are not stored. As with any digital signature, the signed PDF includes the signer's public certificate (name and certificate details).
  • The recent files on the home screen are stored only on your device. You can remove them with the X next to each one or by clearing the app's data.
  • Read aloud: uses your device's text-to-speech engine (on Android, usually Google's). Installed voices work offline; if you choose a voice marked "needs internet", the text being read is sent to that voice's provider (Google) to generate the audio, under its privacy policy.
  • Scan documents: on Android, Google's document scanner (Google Play Services) is used. Photos are processed on the device and the result goes only to PDF Stroke.

Text recognition

To edit the text of a photo or a scan, the app recognizes it on your device with Google ML Kit: the image and the recognized text are not sent to any server. For diagnostics and usage statistics, ML Kit sends technical data to Google: device model and Android version, app version, recognition performance and errors, and an installation identifier that cannot be used to identify you or your device. This data is encrypted and Google does not share it with third parties. More information: data collected by ML Kit.

In the web version (pdfstroke.com) text is recognized in your browser with PaddleOCR: the first time the recognition models are downloaded, and after that everything runs on your device without sending anything.

Web version

The web version has no accounts and no analytics of its own. The free version shows Google AdSense ads: Google may use cookies and identifiers to show and measure them, and in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (you can change it from the privacy link in the notice). If you buy Pro for the browser, the payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy (we do not receive your payment details) and the license code is checked with its service. The daily download count is kept only in your browser. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data about visits (such as the IP address) for security; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Ads (free version of the Android app)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads when the app opens and when you save, share or convert a document. To show them, Google may collect and process the device's advertising ID, the IP address, approximate location derived from it, and data about usage of and interaction with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent the first time you open the app, and you can change it at any time from the Pro window (crown icon) › Ad privacy. More information: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google privacy policy. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.

Pro subscription

The Pro subscription removes ads and the daily download limit. Payment is handled by Google Play; PDF Stroke does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the subscription is active. To cancel it, use Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

The number of downloads per day is counted only on your device.

Permissions

Internet, for ads, the subscription and ML Kit's technical data. On Android 9 or earlier, the app asks for storage permission only to save PDFs to the Documents folder.

If you have any questions, write to the contact address shown on the Google Play listing.
